## The Vision: A Global Settlement Layer

mag3nt. is a payment and settlement network built for the agentic economy and global commerce. We move money using stablecoins, making transactions instant, borderless, and programmable.

- **Pay** — Use a mag3nt. credential to pay any merchant or agent endpoint.
- **Receive** — Spin up a **paylink** to accept payments instantly without a wallet address exchange.
- **Request** — Use paylinks as invoices or simple payment requests in any chat.
- **Settle** — Move value across borders. With integrated on-ramps and off-ramps, mag3nt. handles the entire settlement lifecycle from local currency to stablecoin and back.

## Pro / Yield+ Credentials

The **Pro / Yield+** credential is the premium version of a mag3nt. credential. It is built for people, agents, merchants, and teams that move money often and need more than a simple payment card.

### 1. Boosted Auto-Yield

Idle stablecoin balances can earn yield through integrated non-custodial yield APIs. Pro / Yield+ users receive a higher yield rate, and `$MAG3NT` stakers receive the best available boost.

Your money can keep working while it waits to be spent, requested, or settled.

### 2. Programmable Sub-Credentials

Issue scoped "child" credentials to sub-agents, employees, or customers.

- **Granular Limits**: Set specific spending caps for each sub-credential.
- **Instant Revocation**: Kill any sub-credential at any time without affecting your main account.
- **Auto-Reload**: Automatically top up sub-credentials from a parent credential when they run low.

### 3. Advanced Spend Policies

Control exactly how money moves with programmable rules:

- **Merchant Allowlists**: Restrict spending to specific verified merchants.
- **Velocity Caps**: Limit how much can be spent per hour, day, or transaction.
- **Time Windows**: Allow spending only during specific hours.

### 4. Paylink Business Tools

Turn a paylink into a serious payment request or settlement page:

- **Verified Paylinks**: Show users that the receiver is verified.
- **Branded Checkout**: A cleaner payment page for your business, agent, or service.
- **Receipts & Statements**: Cleaner records for customers, merchants, and accounting.
- **Webhook Notifications**: Get notified instantly when a payment lands.

### 5. Priority Settlement

Pro / Yield+ users get faster settlement handling, higher limits, and better support for high-volume payment flows. This matters when paylinks are used for commerce, invoices, remittance, or recurring agent activity.

### 6. Team & Agent Controls

Manage multiple agents, teammates, or customers across your credentials:

- **Roles and Permissions**: Decide who can issue, fund, freeze, or redeem credentials.
- **Audit Logs**: See exactly who moved money and when.
- **Instant Freeze**: Stop a credential immediately if something looks wrong.

### 7. Flexible Access

You can access Pro / Yield+ in three ways:

- stake enough `$MAG3NT` and unlock it,
- pay monthly in `$MAG3NT` at a discount,
- or pay monthly in USDC at the standard price.

This keeps mag3nt. open to everyone while giving token holders better economics.
